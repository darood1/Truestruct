TRUESTRUCT WEBSITE

1. Upload the contents of this folder to your web host.
2. GitHub Pages: .nojekyll is included.
3. Navigation uses extensionless links as requested. Your host must support clean URLs / HTML fallback. GitHub Pages may require folder-based pages or a routing setup for extensionless paths.
4. IMPORTANT: Add your real business email address. Open assets/js/script.js and replace YOUR_EMAIL@example.com. The contact form then opens the visitor's email client addressed to your business.
5. Replace/add website photographs in /images as required. The supplied TrueStruct logo is already included.
6. Before publishing, add your legal entity/company number and registered office to the footer if applicable, and verify privacy/terms wording for your actual data-processing setup.

Compliance note: the site deliberately does not claim Home Office approval. Sponsor licence approval depends on the organisation's genuine operations, eligibility, HR systems and compliance, not website design alone.
