About TrueStruct

Practical consultancy for real organisations

TrueStruct Consultancy and Training provides business consultancy and professional development support to organisations seeking more consistent systems, stronger governance and better workforce practices.

Our approach

We do not begin with a pre-written package. We look at the organisation, its people, existing documentation and the operational requirement. This allows us to identify gaps without adding unnecessary bureaucracy.

What clients can expect

Our work is designed to be understandable and usable by the people responsible for implementing it. Advice is documented, actions are prioritised and templates are adapted to the organisation rather than presented as one-size-fits-all solutions.

Professional boundaries: TrueStruct provides business consultancy and training. Where legal, immigration, tax or other regulated professional advice is required, clients should obtain advice from an appropriately authorised professional.
